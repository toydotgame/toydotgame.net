## What I Do
Not much. I play Minecraft and occasionally some of the Half-Life games, and I
code _when I can actually come up with ideas_.
This site is mostly dedicated to my code-related doings, however.

## What I've Done in the Past
### 2016
* [A pretty awful Scratch
  account.](https://scratch.mit.edu/users/Minecraft-Game/) This is how I
  discovered software development. Yep.

### 2017
* [More Scratch.](https://scratch.mit.edu/users/Toydotgam/)
* Around this time, I got in to Windows (around build 1709 if you're interested)
  and computer hardware.
* I made my first website, and trust me, it looked like it was straight out of
  the '90s. No, you can't see it.

### 2018
* Nothing much, more Scratch, and more static web dev.

### 2019
* In 2019, I started distro hopping, from Kubuntu, to Ubuntu, to Majaro, back to
  Ubuntu, and then I found Arch…

### 2020
* In 2020, I got a Dell Inspiron 1545 (from like 2009 or 2010 or something) with
  3 GBs of RAM and an awful CPU. This was also my first personal computer that
  only I used.
* In February, I started my Minecraft server,
  [ICCMC](https://iccmc.toydotgame.net/). It started out being hosted on my
  "new" laptop, but as of early 2021 it has its own dedicated PC (an old Lenovo
  ThinkCentre).
* From early-to-mid-2020, I started learning Java.
* This website was launched sometime in the middle of the year.
* In November, I may or may not have accidentally wiped the parition table of my
  hard drive. I bought an SSD to replace it, which was a blessing on such an old
  laptop; and, as I was without any data and had nothing to lose, I might as well
  try Arch, right? Yeah, I still use Arch to this day and I'm never going back.
  So long, Windows 7.
* In December I wrote the concept and base code for my first major Java project
  that I intended to support for a long time, Thisway. I still support and
  develop that plugin to this day.[^1]

[^1]:
	"To this day" was written in 2021/2022-ish. As of *now* (late 2025), I'm in
	the process of sunsetting Thisway with its final major update, as I haven't
	played 1.6.4 nor used Spigot since 2022.

### 2021
* I upgraded my crappy laptop to its max specs, 8 GBs of RAM, the aforementioned
  SSD, and a decent Core 2 Duo.
* In July I rewrote my personal website to what it is today.[^2]
* In October, I started [my first ever browser
  extension](https://github.com/toydotgame/old-google)! It's a Firefox addon
  that achieved the fairly simple goal of changing the Google logo on
  google.com. Like Thisway, I still plan, and _do_, support this addon to this
  day.

[^2]:
	"Today" as of writing that was what I now call site v3. As of writing
	*this* footnote (2025-11-09), we're on v6.

  massive upgrade from what I came from.
* I moved the ICCMC server from the Lenovo ThinkCentre (which now only hosts the
  website) to an old HTPC box from circa 2010.[^3]
* The v4 design of this site is made sometime in November/December (I forget).

### 2023
* I think it's around now that the ICCMC server is updated to *iccmc3*, the
  first custom-built-specifically-for-ICCMC computer
* This website has its "v5" redesign. This also marks the definite end of my
  2019~2022 use of Jekyll (at no point did I actually learn how to do it right
  lol)
* Most of my work is put into academic or entirely personal projects. My
  website, ToyPack, Thisway, and Old Google are still prodded at on occasion,
  however

### 2024
* I get my hands on my first ThinkPad after wanting one for quite a few years.
  It's a T480: "The Last Great ThinkPad;" it was kinda my dream laptop when I
  started wanting it because in \<whenever it was\>, a 2017 laptop wasn't a
  stretch to call "modern"
* The majority of my software-related work shifts to Old Google, this website,
  and my internal management stuff for ICCMC. Albeit a greater quantity of stuff
  done, it's on less things overall—and retrospectively, there isn't anything
  crazy interesting to say about it

### 2025
* I learn Python and [p5.js](https://p5js.org/) for my schooling
* My site gets its *v6* redesign, and I properly invest in migrating everything
  to Jekyll so I can do such redesigns on a whim with far less headache. Plus,
  the `contentloader.js` script that helped make v5 more dynamic couldn't do
  everything (e.g. `<head>`{:.highlight.language-html} tags before parsing)—so
  a proper static site generator was in need.<br>
  I'm not *crazy* on static sites or web stuff in general (surprisingly, given
  my efforts thus far), so I chose Jekyll over the others *purely* because of
  it's GitHub Pages integration. Everything else I could bodge in to get it
  working how I liked :3
* I learn how to make and subsequently release two Minecraft datapacks:
  chestloaders and racecarts++
* ToyPack development resumes after a year-or-two hiatus
* I begin to sunset Thisway after 3+ years without a minor release (planning a
  refactor and final, major release)
