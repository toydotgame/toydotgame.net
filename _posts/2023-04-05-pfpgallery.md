I've had a few profile pictures over the years. I have no idea if or what I had
on my older accounts, but since 2017, most things are relatively
well-documented.

### Ravenclaw (2017~2018)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfpravenclaw.png" />
Not much to say about this one. I was a fan of the Harry Potter books (I have
since discovered _far_ better literature, trust me), and I just downloaded a
generic resource folder from Pottermore after doing one of those house sorting
tests. It's the oldest file I have categorised in my profile picture resource
folder, and once served as my profile picture on both of my Scratch accounts at
the time. Currently, the only remaining account with this profile picture is
Minecraft-Game on Scratch.

### Toydotgam Diamond (2018–2019)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfptoydotdiamond.jpg" />
This image is from a site called Novaskin, where you upload a Minecraft skin
image file and it creates a 3D model that you can use in a lot of predefined
scenes to create images with your own Minecraft skin in it. I thought it was
super cool at the time, and also I wanted a unique-to-me profile picture to
replace the generic one(s) I had had before, so I chose one I liked and went
with it. Sometime in late 2019, I saw a FitMC video on YouTube where the same
Novaskin template was used in the thumbnail with Fit's own skin, and realised
that my profile picture still wasn't unique enough. I had been using Discord for
about six months around this time as well, and I was getting bored of chatting
and seeing my profile picture and its bland colours in the chat.

### Presentation1 (2019–2020, 2020–2021)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfppresentation1.jpg" />
Despite wanting more uniqueness, this profile picture's base was still created
in Novaskin. _But you know what else had happened between 2018 and 2019?_
PewDiePie vs. T-Series. 2019 was the one exclusive year I watched PewDiePie, and
when I say "watched," I mean I watched him almost exclusively for many months on
end. I was the peak cringe 9-year-old (also very active on
/r/PewDiePieSubmissions). So, similar to how my name comes from Logdotzip,
Presentation1 came from PewDiePie. My reference to him is the very obvious
PewDiePie red wavy pattern in the centre covering the "face" part of my profile
picture. But, I was still a Minecraft fan at heart, so I overlayed a
semi-transparent Grass Block texture over it. I still like this profile picture;
it's been my longest-lasting profile picture, spanning over three years.[^1]

[^1]:
	As of doing some minor edits today (2025-11-09), *Presentation1*'s 3-year
	span is *still* the longest of any avatar I've had. However, *4Tech* (2023)
	(my current as of right now) has finally broken the one-new-avatar-a-year
	trend of the preceding few.

	2019–2021 (*Presentation1*) and 2023–2025/present (*4Tech*) are both
	3-calendar-year spans! It's hard to say *exactly* when each avatar had their
	true start and end dates, so I don't know their longevity in days.<br>
	However, give it a few months, and very likely by whenever it is you're
	reading this, (and provided I don't replace *4Tech* any time soon…) it's
	*probably* safe to say that *4Tech* is my *new* longest-lasting profile
	picture.

But why the name? _Presentation1?_ I'll tell you why: I made it in PowerPoint.
Presentation1 is the default PowerPoint presentation name. I still have the
`Presentation1.pptx` file on my computer today. It's a single slide, 1,000px ×
1,000px in size. The center piece over the face is just the wavy thing cropped
to a square with a 6pt border, and a Grass Block of the same size overlayed at
50% opacity or whatever. It turned out alright for something made in PowerPoint.

This profile picture is still in place on my "Toydotgam" account on Scratch.
I'd make a guess that maybe one or two of my other accounts still have it, just
because I had it for such a long time – but honestly, I'm not positive; I think
it only survives on Scratch.

### Picrew (2020)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfppicrew.png" />
In late 2019, I came out as bi; a lot of people I know came out around that
time. And as the stereotype goes, everyone with
<a href="https://picrew.me/ja/secret_image_maker/GWQo0ayzr7bd5eKF" title="Sangled Character Creator on Picrew">one
of those Sangled Picrews</a> was a member of the LGBT community. I wanted in on
being a stereotypical LGBT person lmao, so I made one too. When I say everyone I
knew had one, I mean it. In 2020, 9 out of 10 people I talked on Discord that I
knew had one of these. They eventually died out in my circles, with the final
one that could trace back direct contact to that era being changed only in 2023.

### Voxellate (2021–2022)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfpvoxellate.png" />
Eventually, I grew bored of Presentation1. I also wanted to move on from
PewDiePie, as I was slowly coming to realise that I was, in fact, cringe. Around
this time I had found the GitHub account of someone I once knew, by the name of
[_Voxellate_](https://github.com/Voxellate/). Being around the time I was
considering a new profile picture, I really liked what he had: just a simple,
monotone, pixel-art-y style profile picture that really stood out. So I took my
current Minecraft skin's face, as the pixel art part, and gave it a white
background to inset it (also making a variant with extra padding for circular
profile picture sites), then I made an overlay of a saturated tint of
`#964594`{:.highlight.language-css},the hex for the purple part of the bisexual
pride flag. At the time, I really liked what I had made. This profile picture
survives on my SevenForums and TenForums accounts only AFAIK.

### Voxellate F (2022–2023)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfpvoxellatef.png" />
<a href="https://namemc.com/skin/66f58e66b1c20aa3" title="NameMC - toydotgame Minecraft Skin">I
updated my Minecraft skin in 2022</a>, and so I kind of had to update my profile
picture. The only real difference between my "Voxellate" avatar and Voxellate F
is [obviously] the skin, but also that it had two layers on the skin, so I had
to overlay the "helmet" layer of the skin over the normal "head" layer, and of
course probably the more obvious fact that I removed the saturation of the bi
pride flag purple. At the time of applying it, I thought my new profile picture
looked super grey and desaturated, but looking at it now, my old one just seems
really over-saturated.

<small>A bit of context for the skin change: I had been questioning my gender
since late 2021, and I updated my skin to be a bit more feminine after a few
months. In March of 2023, I came out as genderfluid. I guess that solves that
lmao.</small>

I don't plan on changing this profile picture any time soon, but in the last few
months as of writing, I have been more and more a part of the furry community
(make of that what you want, I don't really care at this point), so I might
replace it with a sona design once I can get GIMP working for digital art lol.
If I make a proper sona, I'd probably update my Minecraft skin as well, but I
don't plan on making my next profile picture a Minecraft-skin-based one – the
profile picture I have will probably just be the straight-up sona design and not
the Minecraft version of it.

### April Fools' 2024 (2024–present)
<img class="fleft" width="30%" src="/media/blog/2023/04-05_pfpgallery/pfpaprilfool.webp" />
For context, on 1 April 2023 I set my Discord profile picture back to *Toydotgam
Diamond* (2018) and pretended it was 1 April 2019. So on 1 April 2024, I did the
same, but a year forward: it's 1 April 2020! As such, my profile picture on
Discord was switched to my Picrew one from 2020. I ended up kinda
re-liking-having a Picrew profile picture again, after 3-odd years of the
*Minecraft face but ourple* format.

So, once the ""gag"" was up, I went out and actually made a *new* Picrew for
myself,[^2] to reflect my 2024 perception of myself rather than the
now-too-different-from-the-present-me 2020 me. I set it as my profile picture
in the subsequent days in early April. As of writing this update (2025-11-09),
it's still my avatar (albeit exclusively on Discord[^3]).
